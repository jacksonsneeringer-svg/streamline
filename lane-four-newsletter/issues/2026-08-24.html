In the Water This Week
Finke pops the question in Rome
Two-time Olympic champion Bobby Finke announced his engagement to longtime partner Ellie Zweifel, a fellow University of Florida alum. The couple shared the news from Rome, which is a pretty solid place to say yes. (via SwimSwam)
Mediterranean Games kicks off with three golds
Apostolos Siskos of Greece opened the 2026 Mediterranean Games with a win in the 200 backstroke, clocking 1:55.95 on Day 1. Pilato and Mancini also topped the podium to give the host region a strong start. (via SwimSwam)
LA 2028 tickets are going fast
The 2028 Los Angeles Olympics has already moved past 6 million tickets sold, and the next chance to buy won't come until early 2027. If you were thinking about going, that window is tighter than it looks. (via SwimSwam)
Texas is loaded and coming back for more
The Texas Longhorns men's team, two-time reigning NCAA Division I champions, are returning the most points of any program heading into the 2027 season. A three-peat is very much on the table. (via SwimSwam)
Athlete of the Week

Thomas Ceccon
Olympic Gold Medalist World Record Holder
World Champion 100m Backstroke Specialist

Thomas Ceccon is an Italian competitive swimmer, born on 27 January 2001 in Thiene, in the province of Vicenza, Italy. He trains under coach Alberto Burlina for Gruppo Sportivo Fiamme Oro and has held the Italian record in the long course 100 metre backstroke since December 2020.

Ceccon is the 2024 Olympic champion and current world record holder in the 100 metre backstroke, having set the world record at the 2022 World Aquatics Championships with a time of 51.60 seconds. He has won four Olympic medals in total, along with three World Championship titles and four European Championship titles in long course competition.
