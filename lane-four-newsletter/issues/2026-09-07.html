In the Water This Week
Regan Smith opens up on Bowman, tapering, and what's next
Regan Smith sat down for a candid conversation about what it's like training under Bob Bowman, her ongoing pursuit of the perfect taper, and the state of U.S. backstroke. She also touched on Kaylee McKeown's season away and how a loaded American backstroke field is pushing everyone to be better. (via SwimSwam)
Nationals drew 18K fans in Irvine -- here's how Pan Pacs compared
USA Swimming released official attendance figures for both events held in Irvine, with U.S. Nationals pulling in 18,701 over five days and Pan Pacs coming in at 13,439. The gap reflects the significantly smaller athlete field at Pan Pacs rather than a drop in fan interest. (via SwimSwam)
Dressel vs. ocean fish: how does his power actually stack up?
In a new "Man vs Fish" challenge, Caeleb Dressel strapped into a harness to measure his pulling power against some massive ocean game fish. It's a fun look at just how much raw strength one of the sport's most explosive swimmers actually generates. (via SwimSwam)
400+ para swimmers head to Turkey for the year's biggest para meet
The Gebze Olympic Swimming Pool in Kocaeli, Turkey will host the 2026 World Para Swimming European Championships, welcoming more than 400 athletes from 38 nations. It stands as the largest para swimming event of the year. (via SwimSwam)
Athlete of the Week

Summer McIntosh
2024 Olympic 3x Gold World Champion
Canadian Record Holder World Record Holder

Summer McIntosh is a Canadian competitive swimmer born on August 18, 2006. The daughter of former Canadian Olympic swimmer Jill Horstead, she grew up competing in both swimming and figure skating before focusing entirely on swimming. She first gained international attention at age 14 as the youngest member of the Canadian team at the 2020 Summer Olympics.

McIntosh is a three-time Olympic champion, eight-time World Aquatics champion, and two-time Commonwealth Games gold medallist. She holds world records in the 200 meter butterfly, 200 and 400 meter individual medley, and 400 meter freestyle. At the 2024 Summer Olympics she won four individual medals, including three gold.
